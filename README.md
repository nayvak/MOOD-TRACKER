#MOOD TRACKER
This is a desktop python application for tracking your moods and organizing your day. In a world where AI has taken over everything, we have no clue on how to process our emotions. Whenever we feel off, we just go on an AI app, and it helps us process everything. But this app allows you to process your emotions without the help of AI.

##FEATURES 
1. **Mood Check-in**: Within this feature, you get to select your mood from the 7 given moods. You can only put one entry in a day. By clicking the previous history button, you can see all the entries you have made with their respective dates.
2. **Journal**: This feature allows you to write about your day and process your emotions. You can also view your previous entries by clicking the previous history button.
3. **Sticky Notes**: On clicking the sticky notes button, you will see a screen where you can write about the important things that you want to remember. It also allows you edit and delete the existing notes.
4. **To-do List**: This feature helps you create a to-do list. You can mark the tasks that you have done and delete them.
5. **Analysis**: The analysis feature provides you with a brief comment on how your mood was throughout the week based on the entries made. It also provides a graph of the entries that you have made.
